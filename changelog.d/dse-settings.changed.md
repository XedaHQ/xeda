- The settings of `xeda dse` take units and have clearer names. Durations and frequencies need a
  unit (`max_runtime: 12 h`, `initial_frequency_low: 100 MHz`). The options
  `--initial-frequency-low` and `--initial-frequency-high` replace `--init-freq-low` and
  `--init-freq-high`, and the old names say what to use. See `xeda dse --help`.
